Privacy Policy – Help the Caterpillar

Effective date: 25 October 2026

In short: Help the Caterpillar is a game made for young children. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal or device data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party tracking, and it plays completely offline. The first 15 levels are free; one optional one-time purchase, made through Google Play behind a grown-up check, unlocks the rest of the game. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

1. Who we are

Help the Caterpillar (the “App”) is a maze game for young children (designed for ages of about 3 to 6) on Android, published by Spirit Games (“we”, “us”, “our”), an independent game developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how. It applies to the App on Google Play and to every version of it.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The App does not collect, request, access or transmit:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details;
  • your precise or approximate location;
  • the advertising ID, Android ID or any other device or user identifier;
  • contacts, photos, videos, files, camera, microphone or calendar data;
  • usage statistics, analytics events, diagnostics or crash reports;
  • payment or financial information (see section 6 on purchases).

3. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the App keeps a small amount of game data in the App’s private storage on your device only:

  • the levels you have completed, the stars and berries you have earned, your bonus-round results and how far you are in the story;
  • your settings: sound effects, music, vibration, screen shake and the difficulty lock;
  • whether the full game has been unlocked.

This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and does not identify you. We cannot see it or access it.

4. How we use information

The on-device data described above is used only to run the game: to let you continue where you left off and to keep your settings. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information with anyone, because we do not collect any.

6. In-app purchase

The first 15 levels of the App are free. A single, optional, one-time in-app purchase (“Full Garden”) unlocks the remaining levels and bonus games. There are no ads, no subscriptions, no virtual currency and nothing else to buy.

  • The purchase screen sits behind a grown-up check (a multiplication question), so a child cannot start a purchase by accident. The App never urges a child to buy.
  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms. We never receive or see your name, email address, payment card or any other payment details.
  • To know whether the game is unlocked, the App asks the Google Play Store app on your device. That answer (unlocked or not) is kept on your device only and is never sent to us.
  • On a child’s Google account managed with Family Link, purchases may need a parent’s approval; the App simply waits for it.

7. Permissions

The App does not have internet access and never connects to the internet itself. It uses:

  • vibration, for the optional gentle buzz when you play (you can turn it off in the App’s settings);
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) and view network state, which are added by Google’s Play Billing Library so the purchase can be made through the Google Play Store app.

None of these are sensitive permissions. The App does not request access to your location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone, advertising ID or any other sensitive data.

8. Advertising and third-party services

The App shows no advertising of any kind and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It is built with the Unity game engine, with Unity’s analytics and online services turned off. The only third-party component is Google’s Play Billing Library, used solely for the optional purchase described above. The App is distributed through Google Play; Google’s own handling of data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

9. Links

The only link in the App is the privacy policy button in its settings, which opens this page in your web browser. The settings, like the purchase screen, open only after a grown-up check. The purchase opens Google Play’s own payment window.

10. Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by using Settings → Reset inside the App (it asks you to confirm first), by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Resetting progress does not remove the purchase: it stays with your Google account and is restored automatically.

11. Security

Your game data is kept in the App’s private storage, protected by Android’s own app sandbox, so other apps cannot read it. Since nothing is transmitted, nothing can be intercepted in transit.

12. Children

The App is designed for children and takes part in Google Play’s Families program. It complies with the Google Play Families Policy and with children’s privacy laws such as the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). It contains no ads, no chat or ways to contact other people, and no links that a child can follow: the settings, where the privacy policy link is, sit behind a grown-up check. The one optional purchase is protected by a grown-up check and paid through Google Play.

13. Your privacy rights

Privacy laws around the world – for example the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil’s LGPD, Canada’s PIPEDA and similar laws elsewhere – give you rights such as access, correction, deletion and objection. Because we do not collect or process any personal data, we hold no data about you to which those rights could apply. If you have any question about your privacy, please contact us and we will answer.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the App were ever to collect any data, this policy would be updated, and the Google Play data-safety information changed, before that version is released.
