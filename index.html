Help the Caterpillar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short: Help the Caterpillar does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it does not use the internet.

Who we are

Help the Caterpillar (the “App”) is a puzzle game for Android developed by Fatih Başal (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

None. The App does not collect, request or transmit any of the following:

  • name, email address, phone number or any other contact details
  • location
  • advertising ID, device ID or any other unique identifier
  • contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone data
  • usage statistics, analytics events or crash reports

The App does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few values only on your own device: the levels you have completed, the stars and berries you have earned, your chosen caterpillar look, and your settings (sound, vibration, screen shake, difficulty). This information never leaves your device, is not shared with anyone and does not identify you. You can delete it at any time with the reset button in the App's settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Advertising

The App shows no advertising of any kind, personalised or otherwise, and contains no advertising SDKs.

Third-party services

The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. It is built with the Unity game engine; Unity’s analytics are disabled and, without internet access, the engine cannot send any data. The App is distributed through Google Play, whose own handling of data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Links

The only link in the App is the privacy policy button in the settings, which opens this page in your web browser.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience. Because it collects no personal data at all, it does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Since we do not collect or process any personal data, we hold no data about you that could be accessed, corrected, transferred or deleted. If you have any questions about your rights under the GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) or any other privacy law, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. If a future version of the App ever begins to collect data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
